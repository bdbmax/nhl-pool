When to take someone

Tap a player to see his chance of still being there at each of your picks, and the last pick where he is still there 8 times out of 10. Tap "Watch this player" to keep that at the top of the list.

Warnings for watched players

A banner at the top warns you about watched players. Blue means plan to take him at your next pick. Yellow means he could go before your next pick. Red means take him now, because he probably will not last until your following pick. Dobeš is watched from the start.

Second goalie

Only your best goalie counts, so a second goalie rarely adds points. Past seasons showed it is better to spend middle picks on skaters and take your second goalie in your last two picks. Once you have one goalie, the Need view hides goalies until then. Watched goalies still get warnings.

Habs bonus

Montreal players get 10 extra points of value for sorting only, so they win close calls. Their projected points are not changed. Change the team or the bonus in Settings.

Saving

Every pick is saved right away. If the browser crashes, open the board again and it continues where you were. Click the draft name at the top to switch drafts or go back in time, and "+ New" to start another draft.

The boxes at the top

Each box shows how many players you have at that position out of how many you must draft. "Falls 12 by your next pick" means the best player left there is expected to be 12 points worse by your next turn. A big fall means take that position now.

Tags

  • PP1: plays on his team's first power-play unit.
  • Injury-prone: played under 80% of games in 2 of the last 3 seasons.
  • IR at camp: on injured reserve now, so 10 games are already removed.
  • Age 33+: higher risk of decline and missed games.
  • Tandem or backup: goalie expected to start under 55% or 35% of games.
  • New team, rookie/import, not in lineup: less history to go on, so less certain.
  • Volatile: his likely range is unusually wide.
